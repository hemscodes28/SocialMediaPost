Data Deletion Instructions

How to request removal of your Post Pilot.ai account, connected social accounts, and stored access tokens.

social.dmprojects.in Last updated: May 23, 2026

1. Your right to delete data

You may request deletion of personal data and platform credentials associated with your Post Pilot.ai account. Upon verified completion of a deletion request, we will remove your stored account record and associated data from our systems, subject to limited legal or security retention requirements.

2. What will be deleted

When your deletion request is processed, the following are permanently removed from Post Pilot.ai:

  • Your user profile (name, email, and login credentials)
  • All connected social media accounts (Instagram, LinkedIn, and related connections)
  • Stored OAuth access tokens and refresh credentials
  • Scheduled posts and job history linked to your account
  • Uploaded media and captions stored on our servers for your account
Permanent action Deletion cannot be undone. You will need to create a new account and reconnect platforms if you wish to use the Service again.

3. How to request deletion

Send an email from the address associated with your Post Pilot.ai account to:

abinayas1411@gmail.com

Please include in your message:

  1. Subject line: “Data Deletion Request — Post Pilot.ai”
  2. The email address registered on your account
  3. Your full name (as shown in the app)
  4. A clear statement that you want all account data deleted
  5. (Optional) Connected platforms to remove (Instagram, LinkedIn, etc.)

We may reply to confirm your identity before processing the request. Deletion is typically completed within 30 days of verification, unless a longer period is required by law.

4. Disconnect before deletion (recommended)

You can immediately stop automated posting by disconnecting accounts in your dashboard (My Profile → connected accounts). You should also revoke Post Pilot.ai access in:

  • Meta / Facebook App Settings
  • LinkedIn Permitted Services

Dashboard disconnect removes tokens from our database; platform-side revocation ensures Meta/LinkedIn no longer honor previously issued tokens.

5. Meta (Facebook) data deletion callback

If you connected via Facebook Login, you may also remove the app from your Facebook settings. For Meta App Review compliance, this page serves as our user-facing data deletion instructions URL:

Data deletion URL: https://social.dmprojects.in/delete-data.html

6. Questions

For status updates on a pending deletion request or privacy questions, contact abinayas1411@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
