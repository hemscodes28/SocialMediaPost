Privacy Policy

How Post Pilot.ai collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our social media automation platform.

social.dmprojects.in Last updated: May 23, 2026

1. Overview

Post Pilot.ai (“we”, “our”, “the platform”) is operated at https://social.dmprojects.in. This policy explains what data we process, why we process it, and the choices you have. By creating an account or connecting a social profile, you agree to this policy.

2. Authentication (OAuth)

We use industry-standard OAuth 2.0 authentication so you can sign in securely without sharing your platform passwords with us. Depending on the feature you use, you may authenticate via:

  • Email and password or Google (Firebase Authentication)
  • Meta / Facebook Login (for Instagram publishing)
  • LinkedIn OAuth (for LinkedIn publishing)

OAuth providers issue short-lived authorization codes; we exchange these for access tokens only to perform actions you explicitly request (such as publishing or scheduling a post).

3. Access tokens & secure storage

When you connect Instagram, LinkedIn, or related services, we store the minimum credentials required to act on your behalf—typically access tokens, account identifiers, and connection metadata (e.g. username, page name, token expiry).

  • Tokens are stored in our application database on secured infrastructure.
  • Tokens are transmitted only over HTTPS.
  • We do not display full tokens in the user interface.
  • Tokens are used solely to provide posting, scheduling, and account-management features you initiate.
We do not sell or rent your tokens Access tokens and OAuth credentials are never sold, licensed, or shared with advertisers or data brokers.

4. How connected accounts are used

Connected social media accounts are used only for automated posting and related operations you authorize, including:

  • Publishing posts, carousels, and captions to Instagram or LinkedIn
  • Scheduling content for future publication
  • Refreshing tokens when required by the platform provider
  • Displaying connection status in your dashboard

We do not use your connected accounts to read private messages, access unrelated personal data, or perform actions outside the scope of Post Pilot.ai features.

5. Other information we may collect

  • Account data: name, email address, and profile preferences.
  • Content you upload: images, captions, and scheduling metadata necessary to fulfill your requests.
  • Technical logs: IP address, browser type, and error logs for security and reliability (retained for a limited period).
  • Analytics: aggregated usage metrics to improve the product (where enabled).

6. Data sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for their marketing purposes. We may share data only when:

  • Required to operate the service (e.g. API calls to Meta, LinkedIn, or hosting providers under strict confidentiality)
  • Required by law or valid legal process
  • Necessary to protect the security and rights of users and the platform

7. Disconnecting accounts

You may disconnect any linked social account at any time from your Post Pilot.ai dashboard. Disconnecting stops future automated actions for that account. You may also revoke app access directly in your Meta, LinkedIn, or Google account security settings.

To request full deletion of your stored account data, see our Data Deletion page.

8. Retention & security

We retain data only as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes. We apply reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards; no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.
